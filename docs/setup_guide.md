# Setup guide (patient and physiotherapist)

1. Charge the belt through USB-C until the charger LED shows full.
2. Wear the belt snugly without restricting breathing, module centered on the lower back.
3. The physiotherapist positions the patient in correct posture (sitting or standing).
4. Hold the calibrate button for 2 seconds: three blinks and beeps confirm the baseline is saved.
5. Normal use: vibration warns first, the buzzer follows if posture is not corrected.
6. Adjust angle limits and delays in the firmware settings to suit the patient.
